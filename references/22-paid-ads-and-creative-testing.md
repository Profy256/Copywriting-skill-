# 22 - Paid Ads and Creative Testing Systems

Paid media punishes weak copy and vague targeting quickly. This file covers account logic, creative systems, copy per platform, testing, and budget discipline, with small-budget and East Africa realities in mind. Platform specs and policies change; verify current limits and rules.

## 1. Principles

- **Offer and angle beat design tweaks.** Test them first.
- **The creative is the targeting** on modern platforms: the message attracts the right audience.
- **One ad, one idea, one action.**
- **Know the unit economics before you spend:** price, margin, conversion rates, and what a customer is worth.
- **Message match** from ad to page to conversation.
- **Start small, learn fast, scale what works.**

## 2. Objectives and funnel structure

| Funnel stage | Objective | Creative style | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold (unaware/problem aware) | Reach/engagement/traffic/leads | Problem hooks, education, story, entertainment | Hook rate, CTR, cost per landing view/lead |
| Warm (solution/product aware) | Conversions, messages | Proof, comparison, demo, offer | Cost per message/lead/purchase |
| Hot (retargeting) | Purchase/booking | Objection handling, urgency, guarantee, testimonials | CPA, ROAS |
| Existing customers | Retention, referrals, upsell | Loyalty, new products, referral offers | Repeat rate, LTV |

For small local budgets: often **one campaign with 2 to 3 ad sets and 3 to 6 creatives** aimed at messages or leads is enough.

## 3. The creative matrix (angles x formats x hooks)

Create variety systematically:

- **Angles** (from `03`): outcome, pain, speed, effort, cost, risk, identity, mechanism, story, contrast, seasonal, locality.
- **Formats** (from `14`): demo, testimonial, UGC, before/after, myth-busting, POV skit, explainer, founder-to-camera, listicle/carousel, comparison.
- **Hooks:** 3 variants per angle (question, bold claim, scene).

Example grid: 3 angles x 2 formats x 2 hooks = 12 creatives. Launch the best 4 to 6; keep the rest in reserve for refresh.

## 5. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads

- **Structure:** primary text (hook in the first 1 to 2 lines), headline (short, benefit/offer), description (optional), CTA button.
- **Placements:** feed (4:5), Stories/Reels (9:16), Marketplace; design for sound-off and safe zones.
- **Copy patterns:** PAS, BAB, testimonial, offer-led, story; see `06`.
- **Click-to-WhatsApp/Messenger:** prefilled message text ("Hi, I want the price list for [product]"); instant greeting; fast human reply.
- **Lead forms:** short; qualifying questions to reduce junk; follow up fast.
- **Audience:** broad with strong creative often works; narrow by location (city/area), age, language, and interests where useful; exclude existing customers when needed.
- **Optimisation:** choose the objective aligned with the business outcome (messages, leads, purchases); allow the learning phase before judging.

## 6. Google Search, Performance Max and Display/YouTube

- **Search:** intent-led; write ads for keyword groups; headline mix of keyword, benefit, offer, proof, CTA, location; strong extensions (sitelinks, callouts, call, location); negative keywords; landing page with message match.
- **Performance Max/Display:** requires diverse assets (headlines, descriptions, images, videos); give the system clear brand and audience signals; monitor placements and quality.
- **YouTube:** hook in the first 5 seconds before skip; show the product early; clear CTA; use customer language; vertical for Shorts.
- **Local:** location extensions and call-only for service businesses.

## 7. TikTok and short video ads

- Native, vertical, fast cuts, human face, captions, trending-sound awareness (licensing rules apply for business accounts).
- **Structure:** Hook (0-3s) -> Problem/context -> Demo/proof -> Offer -> CTA.
- **Creator-style** (UGC) tends to outperform polished ads; avoid obviously "ad" openings.
- Test 3 hooks on the same body.

## 8. WhatsApp-driven ad flows

Where sales close in chat, optimise the **entire** chain:
1. **Ad** promises a clear thing and invites a specific message.
2. **Prefilled message** includes product or offer code to track source.
3. **Auto-greeting** acknowledges, sets expectations, and asks a qualifying question.
4. **Human reply** within minutes with price, proof, and the next step.
5. **Follow-up** cadence (see `08`).
6. **Measurement:** conversations started, qualified chats, sales, and cost per sale, using tags, codes, or manual notes.

## 9. Retargeting and lookalikes

- **Retargeting** audiences: website visitors, video viewers, page engagers, WhatsApp/lead lists (where permitted), cart abandoners.
- Creative: reminder + objection handling + proof + guarantee + deadline (real).
- **Frequency control** to avoid annoyance.
- **Lookalikes/similar audiences** from customer lists (consent and platform rules apply) to find new buyers.
- Exclude recent purchasers unless offering a relevant next product.

## 10. Testing system for small budgets

- Test **big variables first:** offer, angle, audience, format, hook.
- **Budget rule of thumb:** each creative needs enough spend to produce a meaningful signal, such as a few times your target cost per result; judge on cost per quality result, not just clicks.
- **Kill rule:** pause ads that exceed 2 to 3 times the target cost per result without quality signals after the learning period.
- **Scale rule:** increase budgets gradually (about 20 to 30 percent steps) on winners; duplicate winning ads into new audiences.
- Keep one **control** ad and challenge it with variants.
- Maintain a **testing log** (hypothesis, creative, results, learning).
- When volume is too low for statistical rigor, use **directional decisions** plus qualitative feedback from replies and comments.

## 11. Budgeting and unit economics

Compute before spending:
- **Gross margin per sale** = price - cost of goods/delivery/fees.
- **Allowable acquisition cost (CPA)** = a share of margin (or of lifetime value).
- **Break-even ROAS** = 1 / margin rate.
- **Conversion chain:** impressions -> clicks -> chats/leads -> qualified -> sales; estimate each rate to forecast spend needed.
- **LTV:** repeat purchases, referrals; allows higher CPA if justified.
- **Cash flow:** payment timing, delivery costs, refunds.
Advise honestly if the economics do not work (price too low, margin too thin, conversion too weak) before recommending ad spend.

## 12. Landing and conversation alignment

- Ad promise = page headline = chat greeting.
- Page loads fast on mobile; CTA obvious; WhatsApp button prefilled.
- Offers consistent across platforms (same price, same terms, same deadline).
- Prepare **sales replies** and **FAQ answers** for the questions the ad will provoke.

## 13. Creative fatigue and refresh

- Signs: rising frequency, dropping CTR, rising CPA, comments saying "saw this already".
- Refresh with new hooks (same body), new formats, new proof, new offer angles, and seasonal updates.
- Keep a **creative pipeline**: always 2 to 3 new concepts in production.

## 14. Ad compliance

- Follow platform policies (special categories, health, finance, employment, housing, politics, alcohol, gambling) and local laws.
- No misleading claims, fake before/after, personal-attribute call-outs, sensational imagery, or false urgency.
- Disclose partnerships and sponsorship as required.
- Use rights-cleared music, images, and fonts.
- Keep landing pages consistent and transparent (policies, contact, pricing).

## 15. Reporting template

```markdown
# Ad report: [period]
Spend | Conversations/leads | Sales | CPA | ROAS
Top creatives (why they worked) | Losers (why)
Audience and placement insights
Customer language gained (comments, replies)
Learnings and next tests
Budget recommendation
```
