## 1. The audit mindset

- **Diagnose before prescribing.** A weak page can come from the wrong traffic, the wrong offer, a trust gap, a technical fault, or weak copy. Find the constraint.
- **Evidence over taste.** Use analytics, recordings, support messages, reviews, and user tests where available. If you have none, say which findings are expert judgement versus data.
- **Fix the biggest leak first.** Improving a step that few people reach changes little.
- **Be honest.** If the product, price, or traffic is the problem, say so and show why.

## 2. Audit method (step by step)

1. **Goal and numbers:** primary conversion (sale, lead, booking, WhatsApp chat), current conversion rate, traffic by source, average order value, margin, and the target.
2. **First impression test (5 seconds):** show the first screen to someone unfamiliar; ask what it is, who it is for, and what they would do next. Record answers verbatim.
3. **Read as the customer:** walk the full path from the ad or search result to the thank-you page, on a phone, on slow data, as a skeptical first-timer.
4. **Message match:** does the page continue the promise made in the ad, post, or search snippet?
5. **Clarity and value:** headline, subhead, offer, price, proof, CTA. Apply the scorecard in `10-quality-and-testing.md`.
6. **Friction and anxiety:** list every moment of doubt, effort, or confusion.
7. **Trust:** identify missing proof (section 7).
8. **Technical:** speed, broken elements, mobile layout, tracking.
9. **Competitive comparison:** put the page next to two competitors; what would make a visitor choose them?
10. **Hypotheses:** for each finding, write "If we change X, then Y will improve because Z".
11. **Prioritise** (section 11) and **recommend a test plan**.

## 3. The diagnostic frameworks

**LIFT model** (conversion drivers and killers): **Value proposition** (the central driver), **Relevance** (match to visitor and source), **Clarity** (understandable, scannable, obvious next step), **Urgency** (a real reason to act now), **Anxiety** (doubts and fears; reduce), **Distraction** (anything pulling attention from the goal; remove).

**Conversion equation (informal):** Conversion = Motivation + Value clarity - Friction - Anxiety (+ Incentive/urgency). Raise motivation (better offer, better targeting), raise clarity, lower friction and anxiety.

**Fogg behaviour model:** Behaviour happens when **Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt** converge. Check each: do visitors want it, can they do it easily, and is the prompt (CTA) clear and timely?

**Jobs-to-be-done lens:** what job did the visitor hire this page for? Does the page help them make progress on that job?

**Awareness lens:** is the page written for the awareness level of the traffic? (A level-1 visitor on a price-led page bounces.)

## 4. Page-level checklist

**Above the fold (mobile first)**
- [ ] Clear headline with outcome for a specific person
- [ ] Subhead adds who, how, or proof
- [ ] Primary CTA visible without scrolling; single main action
- [ ] Proof element visible (rating, number, guarantee, logos, photo)
- [ ] Relevant image or video showing product or outcome
- [ ] Price or price signal (or reason it is hidden)

**Body**
- [ ] Reader's situation described accurately
- [ ] Benefits before features; features as proof
- [ ] Objections answered near the relevant claims
- [ ] Specific, believable proof (named customers, numbers, demos)
- [ ] How it works in 3 to 5 simple steps
- [ ] Offer stack and guarantee explicit
- [ ] FAQ addresses real objections
- [ ] Scannable: subheads, bullets, short paragraphs

**Close**
- [ ] Final CTA with recap and reassurance
- [ ] Contact options visible (WhatsApp, phone, address)
- [ ] Policies reachable (returns, delivery, privacy)
- [ ] No dead ends; next step is always obvious

## 5. Message match and traffic sources

- Compare the **ad or post** and the **landing page** headline, offer, images, and tone. A mismatch is a top cause of waste.
- **Search traffic:** page must answer the query immediately.
- **Social traffic:** often low-intent and mobile; need a faster, simpler page or direct WhatsApp path.
- **Email/WhatsApp traffic:** warm; skip basic explanations, lead with the offer and the next step.
- **Referral traffic:** reflect the referrer's promise and name them if allowed.
- Consider **separate landing pages per segment or offer** when intent differs.

## 6. Forms, checkout and payment friction

- Ask only for what you need; every extra field costs conversions.
- Use clear labels, helpful examples, inline validation, and plain error messages.
- Offer guest checkout; show progress; save carts.
- Show **total cost early** (delivery, tax, fees). Surprise costs kill checkout.
- Offer the payment methods the audience actually uses (mobile money, card, bank, pay on delivery) and show how each works.
- Make payment steps explicit on mobile money: amount, merchant name/code, reference, expected confirmation time.
- Add reassurance beside the pay button: refund policy, secure payment, support contact.
- Provide an escape hatch: "Prefer to order on WhatsApp?" for customers who distrust forms.
- Confirmation page: confirm the order, what happens next, when, and how to reach you.

## 8. Mobile and speed audit

- Test on a mid-range Android phone on a mobile connection.
- First meaningful screen in a few seconds; compress images; avoid heavy sliders and autoplay video.
- Tap targets large; sticky WhatsApp/call button; no horizontal scroll.
- Text readable without zoom; line length short; contrast sufficient.
- Forms use correct keyboards (numeric for phone, email for email).
- Avoid pop-ups that cover content on small screens.

## 9. Funnel and journey mapping

Map the path as stages. For each stage, define: **customer state, question in their head, our job, asset needed, metric.**

| Stage | Customer state | Their question | Our job | Assets | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aware | Has a problem, may not know solutions | "Why is this happening?" | Be found, create recognition | SEO guides, social, radio, referrals | Reach, traffic |
| Interested | Exploring options | "What are my options?" | Educate, differentiate | Comparison pages, videos, lead magnet | Engagement, sign-ups |
| Consider | Comparing providers | "Why you?" | Prove and reduce risk | Case studies, reviews, demo, price | Enquiries, quote requests |
| Decide | Ready but wary | "Is it safe? What do I do?" | Make yes easy | Offer, guarantee, payment steps, chat | Conversion rate |
| Purchase | Paying | "Did it work?" | Confirm, reassure | Confirmation, receipts, onboarding | Completion rate |
| Use | Experiencing the product | "Am I using it right?" | Deliver value fast | Onboarding, how-tos, support | Activation, satisfaction |
| Retain | Considering reorder | "Is it still worth it?" | Remind, reward | Check-ins, loyalty, content | Repeat rate |
| Advocate | Happy | "Who should I tell?" | Ask and reward | Review asks, referral offers | Reviews, referrals |

Draw the funnel with real numbers: visitors -> leads -> chats -> quotes -> sales -> repeat. Find the biggest drop and diagnose that step first.

## 10. Lifecycle messaging map

For each lifecycle trigger, define the message, channel, timing and goal.

| Trigger | Channel | Message goal | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| New lead | WhatsApp/email | Fast helpful reply | Answer + question + next step |
| Quote sent, no reply | WhatsApp | Overcome hesitation | Proof + deadline + easy yes |
| Abandoned cart | Email/WhatsApp | Recover | Reminder + help + guarantee |
| First purchase | Email/WhatsApp | Reassure + onboard | Thanks + how to use + support |
| Day 7 after purchase | Message | Ensure success, ask review | "Is it working? 1-tap review" |
| Day 30 | Message | Cross-sell/reorder | Related item or refill |
| Dormant 60-90 days | Message | Win back | New reason + small offer |
| Birthday/anniversary | Message | Relationship | Personal greeting + gift |
| Complaint | Phone/message | Recover trust | Own it, fix it, follow up |

## 11. Prioritising fixes (ICE)

Score each proposed change 1 to 10 on **Impact** (how much could it lift the key metric), **Confidence** (how sure, from evidence), **Ease** (how cheap and fast). Priority = (I + C + E) / 3 or I x C x E. Do the high-scoring items first; bundle quick wins; reserve tests for uncertain high-impact ideas.

## 13. Quick wins that usually work

- Rewrite the hero around the customer outcome with one clear CTA.
- Add a visible WhatsApp/call button, sticky on mobile.
- Show price or a "from" price; explain what drives price.
- Replace generic claims with specific numbers and examples.
- Add 3 to 5 real, specific testimonials with names and photos.
- Add a plain-language guarantee near the CTA.
- Cut the form to the minimum fields.
- Fix mismatches between ad and landing page.
- Compress images and remove heavy scripts.
- Answer the top 5 objections in an FAQ and near the CTA.
- Fix typos, broken links, and inconsistent contact details.
- Add an address, map, and owner photo for local businesses.
