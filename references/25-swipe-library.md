## 1. Headline patterns (by type)

**Benefit / outcome**
- Get [result] in [time], even if [obstacle]
- [Result] without [pain / cost / hassle]
- The simple way to [result] for [specific person]
- Finally: [result] that [differentiator]
- [Number] [people/units] [achieved result] in [time]

**Problem / pain callout**
- Tired of [specific frustration]? Here's the fix.
- For [specific person] who [specific struggle]
- Why [common problem] keeps happening (and what ends it)
- If [symptom], read this before [costly action]
- Stop [costly habit]. Start [better habit].

**How-to**
- How to [result] in [time/steps]
- How [person like you] [achieved result] using [method]
- How to [result] even if [objection]
- How to choose [product] without getting ripped off

**Number / list**
- [N] mistakes that cost [audience] [money/time]
- [N] questions to ask before you buy [product]
- [N] signs it's time to [action]
- The [N]-minute [routine/check/fix] for [result]

**Question**
- What if [desirable outcome] took [short time]?
- Are you making these [N] [category] mistakes?
- Still [costly behaviour]?
- Is your [asset] helping or hurting [goal]?

**Proof / social proof**
- [N] [customers] chose us in [period]. Here's why.
- "[Specific quote about result]" - [Name, place]
- Rated [X/5] by [N] [customers] in [place]
- From [before] to [after] in [time]: [Customer]'s story

**Offer-led**
- [Offer] for [audience], until [real date]
- Free [valuable thing] with every [purchase], this [week]
- Try [product] for [period]. Pay only if [condition]

**News / new**
- New: [capability] for [audience]
- Now you can [desirable action] by [easy method]
- Introducing [name]: the [category] that [differentiator]

**Curiosity (with a payoff you deliver)**
- The [unexpected thing] that decides whether [outcome]
- Why our [lowest-priced item] is our best seller
- What [experts/insiders] do before [action] that you don't
- The mistake 8 out of 10 [people] make with [thing] (check yours)

**Contrast / comparison**
- [Option A] costs [X]. [Option B] costs [Y]. Guess which lasts longer.
- Cheaper than [alternative], faster than [alternative]
- Not [common approach]. This: [better approach].

**Identity / belonging**
- For [group] who refuse to [compromise]
- Built by [credible type] for [specific audience]
- The [category] for people who [value/behaviour]

**Fear / consequence (use honestly)**
- Every [time unit] without [solution] costs you [real amount]
- What happens to your [asset] if [risk event]?
- Don't wait until [bad moment] to [action]

**Command**
- Stop losing [customers/money] to [cause]
- Get your [thing] sorted before [deadline]
- Book [service] today. Pay after [condition].

## 2. Subheads and deck lines

- [Who it's for] + [how it works] + [proof]: "For busy shop owners. Set up in 10 minutes. Used by 300+ Kampala shops."

- "No contracts. No hidden fees. Cancel anytime."
- "Pay by MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. Delivered in [time]."
- "Backed by our [guarantee]."

## 7. Guarantee and risk-reversal lines

- If [promise] doesn't happen in [time], we [refund / redo / pay you].
- Open or refunded: if your file doesn't open, we replace it in an hour or refund you.
- Pay only after you've seen it work.
- Inspect it before you pay.
- If you're not happy with the first [unit], we'll fix it free.
- Cheaper elsewhere? Show us the written quote and we'll [match / beat it]. (only if policy is real)
- Try it for [period]. Love it or return it.
- We're so sure, we put it in writing: [guarantee].

## 8. Price reveal and value-framing lines

- Just [price]. That's [price per unit/day].
- Less than [familiar local expense].
- Instead of [high-cost alternative] ([price]), get [your option] for [price].
- Starting at [price], with [included items].
- The total is [price]. No delivery fee, no surprises.
- You'll pay [amount] now and [amount] on [date].
- Why is it [price]? Because [honest reason].

## 9. Objection-handling one-liners

- "Too expensive": "Compared to what? If it's [alternative], here's the difference..."
- "I'll think about it": "Of course. What would help you decide?"
- "Is it genuine?": "Yes. Here's [proof]. You can [inspect/verify] before you pay."
- "Will it work for me?": "It depends on [factor]. Tell me [question], and I'll tell you honestly."
- "I've been scammed before": "That's understandable. Here's how we make it safe: [steps]."
- "Competitor is cheaper": "They may be. Here's what's different: [value, risk, service]."
- "No time": "That's exactly why we [save-time mechanism]."
- "Need to ask [person]": "Makes sense. Here's a short summary you can forward."

## 18. Local-flavour hooks (use with care)

- Anchor to real local places, routines, seasons, and costs: "Before the next jam at [junction]", "Term 1 starts on [date]", "Cheaper than a boda to town and back".
- Use local-language greetings or thank-yous only with native review and only when natural.
- Avoid stereotypes and forced slang.

## 19. Power-word families (use specifics first)

Use sparingly; specifics beat adjectives.
- **Clarity:** exactly, step-by-step, plain, simple, clear, checklist, template
- **Speed:** today, in minutes, same-day, instant, ready, within [time]
- **Trust:** guaranteed (only if true), verified, original, official, receipt, tested, registered
- **Value:** save, free, included, bonus, fair, no hidden fees, total
- **Ease:** easy, done-for-you, no setup, just reply, one tap
- **Belonging:** join, members, community, for people like you
- **Curiosity:** secret (careful), why, what, truth, behind, mistake, myth

## 20. Banned or suspect phrases

"World-class", "cutting-edge", "state-of-the-art", "best-in-class", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "robust", "synergy", "leverage" (as a verb for "use"), "unlock", "elevate", "empower", "next-level", "one-stop shop", "passionate team", "customer-centric", "quality service", "we pride ourselves", "look no further", "in today's fast-paced world", "take your business to the next level", "unparalleled", "innovative solutions", "best price in town" (unproven), "number one" (unproven), "guaranteed results" (unsupported). Replace with a fact, a number, a demonstration, or a customer's own words.
