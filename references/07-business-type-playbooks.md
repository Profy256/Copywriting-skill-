# 07 - Business-Type Playbooks

Use after research. Each entry gives the buyer's psychology, the strongest angles, must-have proof, typical objections, best channels, and common mistakes. These are starting hypotheses; research and the brief override them.

## Physical / local

### 1. Restaurant, cafe, bar
- **Psychology:** decision is fast, emotional, social and local; photos and reviews decide.
- **Angles:** signature dish, ambience/occasion, speed and value lunch, group deals, location/view, cleanliness, ingredients/provenance.
- **Proof:** food photos, ratings, queues, health certificate, chef or owner story, regular customers.
- **Objections:** "Is it clean?", "Is it worth the price?", "Will it take long?", "Parking?"
- **Channels:** Google Maps, Instagram/TikTok food video, WhatsApp orders, delivery apps, signage, radio (events).
- **Copy musts:** appetising sensory menu descriptions, clear prices, hours, directions with landmark, how to order/reserve.
- **Mistakes:** dark or amateur food photos, no price, only "tasty", outdated hours.

### 2. Salon, barber, spa
- **Psychology:** trust in skill and hygiene; appearance and confidence; appointment friction.
- **Angles:** specialism (natural hair, locs, kids), transformation, time-slot reliability, products used, hygiene, special-occasion packages.
- **Proof:** before/after, stylist profiles, reviews, portfolio, years, product brands.
- **Objections:** "Will it damage my hair?", "How long?", "How much exactly?"
- **Channels:** Instagram/TikTok transformations, WhatsApp booking, Google Maps, referral.
- **Musts:** price menu with durations, booking link/number, aftercare tips, lateness policy.
- **Mistakes:** hiding prices, no portfolio, slow replies.

### 3. Clinic, pharmacy, dental
- **Psychology:** fear, urgency, trust, privacy; cost anxiety.
- **Angles:** qualified staff, same-day appointments, gentle care, transparent pricing, payment plans, privacy, location and hours.
- **Proof:** licences and registration numbers, practitioner credentials, equipment, hygiene, patient stories (with consent).
- **Objections:** "Is it safe?", "Is it expensive?", "Will it hurt?"
- **Rules:** do not guarantee cures; avoid before/after claims you cannot support; follow the regulator's advertising rules; no "miracle" language; protect patient privacy.
- **Mistakes:** hype claims, unclear prices, no emergency instructions.

### 4. School, tuition, training
- **Psychology:** parents buy peace of mind and a future; students buy progress and belonging; fees are heavy.
- **Angles:** results (exam stats, if real), safety, teachers, small classes, discipline, values, transport/boarding, scholarships and payment plans, career outcomes.
- **Proof:** pass rates, alumni, teacher credentials, accreditation, facility photos, testimonials.
- **Objections:** "Can we afford it?", "Is it safe?", "Will my child get attention?"
- **Channels:** open day, WhatsApp parent groups, radio, local referrals, Facebook, flyers.
- **Musts:** fee structure and instalments, admissions timeline, visit invitation, safeguarding statement.
- **Mistakes:** inflated results, vague "excellence", no price clarity.

### 5. Retail shop, boutique, supermarket
- **Psychology:** convenience, trust in price and quality, habit.
- **Angles:** range and availability, price comparisons, freshness, delivery, loyalty, "everything for [situation]", fast service.
- **Proof:** real photos, price lists, reviews, receipts, years in the area.
- **Channels:** WhatsApp catalogue and status, Facebook Marketplace, signage, flyers, radio, SMS to loyalty list.
- **Musts:** price tags, hours, location, payment modes (cash, MoMo, card), delivery zones.
- **Mistakes:** stock photos, no price, no stock clarity.

### 6. Hardware and building supplies
- **Psychology:** big-ticket purchases, risk of fake or low-quality items, need for advice.
- **Angles:** genuine brands, delivery to site, credit for repeat contractors, bulk discounts, technical advice, stock availability.
- **Proof:** standards marks, brand authorisations, projects supplied, warehouse photos.
- **Objections:** "Is it original?", "Can you deliver today?", "Can I get credit?"
- **Channels:** WhatsApp catalogue by category, signage, contractor networks, Facebook groups, radio.
- **Musts:** price lists, delivery terms, warranty, specification sheets.

### 7. Real estate, rentals, lodging
- **Psychology:** very high trust threshold; fear of fraud; emotional and financial stakes.
- **Angles:** verified titles/papers, location benefits (commute, schools, security), payment plans, transparency on fees, viewing process, lifestyle.
- **Proof:** documents, registered company, site visits, previous projects, testimonials, video walkthroughs.
- **Objections:** "Is this land genuine?", "Hidden fees?", "Is it safe?"
- **Rules:** be accurate on size, price, title type and approvals; no false scarcity.
- **Musts:** clear specifications, price, location with directions, photo gallery, viewing CTA, due-diligence checklist.

### 8. Gym and fitness
- **Psychology:** motivation fades; identity, results, accountability.
- **Angles:** transformation stories, community, trainers, flexible passes, women-only hours, trial week.
- **Proof:** member results (with consent), trainer credentials, equipment photos.
- **Rules:** avoid body-shaming and unrealistic promises.
- **Musts:** trial offer, timetable, pricing tiers, location and parking.

### 9. Garage, car dealer, transport and logistics
- **Psychology:** distrust of mechanics, fear of overcharging, urgency; for logistics: reliability and safety.
- **Angles:** diagnostic transparency, repair warranty, same-day service, genuine parts, upfront quotes, tracking, insured cargo.
- **Proof:** licences, insurance, certifications, tracking screenshots, repeat clients, workshop photos.
- **Objections:** "Will they scam me?", "Will it be fixed right?"
- **Musts:** written quotes, warranty terms, emergency contact, coverage areas.

### 10. Agriculture and agribusiness
- **Psychology:** margin pressure, weather and price risk, need for reliable inputs and markets.
- **Angles:** yield, reliability of supply, freshness and traceability, bulk and contract options, extension support, fair pricing and prompt payment.
- **Proof:** farm photos, certifications, lab tests, buyer testimonials, volumes, harvest schedule.
- **Channels:** WhatsApp groups, local-language radio, cooperatives, demo plots, agro-dealer networks, B2B outreach.
- **Musts:** clear specs (grade, moisture, packaging), seasonal calendar, minimum order, delivery terms.

### 11. Events, photography, weddings
- **Psychology:** one-time emotional event; fear of failure on the day.
- **Angles:** peace of mind, packages, portfolio style, reliability, backup plans, turnaround time.
- **Proof:** full galleries, real client stories, reviews, vendor partnerships.
- **Musts:** named packages with prices or "from" pricing, deposit and cancellation terms, availability, process timeline.

### 12. Phone and electronics sales and repair
- **Psychology:** fear of fakes and of data loss; speed matters.
- **Angles:** original devices and warranty, repair while you wait, price transparency, data safety, trade-in.
- **Proof:** warranty cards, IMEI/serial verification, repair before/after, reviews.
- **Musts:** price list, warranty terms, what is checked on delivery, location.

### 13. Professional services (legal, accounting, consulting)
- **Psychology:** risk, complexity, credibility; they buy certainty.
- **Angles:** specialisation, clear fees, speed, plain-language explanations, risk avoided, compliance peace of mind.
- **Proof:** qualifications and registrations, case outcomes (within confidentiality), client types, publications.
- **Rules:** respect professional advertising rules and confidentiality; do not guarantee outcomes.
- **Musts:** clear scope and fees, first-step offer (free 15-minute call, checklist, audit), FAQs.

### 14. Construction and contractors
- **Psychology:** fear of delays, cost overruns, poor workmanship.
- **Angles:** fixed quotes, timelines, supervision, warranties, licensed engineers, visible past projects.
- **Proof:** completed project photos, client references (with permission), registrations, equipment, safety record.
- **Musts:** a clear process (site visit, quote, contract, milestones), payment schedule, aftercare.

### 15. Fashion and tailoring
- **Psychology:** identity, fit, occasion, status; fear of fit or quality mismatch.
- **Angles:** custom fit, fabric quality, occasion collections, deadlines met, local culture and style.
- **Proof:** lookbooks, customer photos, fitting guarantees, honoured deadlines.
- **Musts:** size guides, lead times, alteration policy, price ranges, delivery/pickup.

### 16. Tourism and hospitality
- **Psychology:** inspiration, safety, value, uniqueness.
- **Angles:** experiences, local expertise, safety record, transparent inclusions, flexible bookings, group packages.
- **Proof:** reviews on international platforms, licences, guide credentials, photo and video.
- **Musts:** itinerary, what is and is not included, cancellation policy, how to book and pay (international options).

### 17. Churches, NGOs, community organisations
- **Psychology:** belonging, purpose, trust in leadership; donors need accountability.
- **Angles:** mission and impact stories, transparency, how gifts are used, community voices.
- **Proof:** reports, audited accounts where available, photos with consent, named beneficiaries (with consent), partners.
- **Rules:** dignity-first storytelling; no pressure or guilt; be transparent about funds.

## Online

### 18. E-commerce and social commerce
- **Psychology:** trust and risk dominate; price comparison is easy.
- **Angles:** authenticity guarantees, delivery speed and cost, easy returns, pay on delivery, real photos and videos, bundles, curation for a niche.
- **Proof:** reviews with photos, delivery times, return stats, social proof.
- **Musts:** product detail, delivery and returns upfront, payment options (mobile money, card), size/fit help, WhatsApp support.
- **Mistakes:** manufacturer copy, hidden shipping costs, no reviews, slow replies.
- **Flow:** ad -> product page/catalogue -> WhatsApp or checkout -> confirmation -> delivery updates -> review request -> repeat offer.

### 19. Marketplaces
- Two audiences: supply and demand. Write separate messages (sellers: earnings, ease, reach, trust; buyers: selection, safety, price).
- Trust copy is crucial: verification, ratings, dispute process, escrow/protection.
- Solve chicken-and-egg with a clear niche and a seeded supply promise.

### 20. SaaS and apps
- **Psychology:** fear of switching and wasted setup time; buyer may not be user.
- **Angles:** job-to-be-done outcome, time saved, error reduction, integrations, ease of onboarding, free trial, security and uptime, local context (mobile money, offline).
- **Proof:** case studies with numbers, logos, usage stats, security attestations, demo video, public roadmap.
- **Musts:** headline = outcome for a role ("Reconcile mobile money payments in minutes"), 3-step "how it works", captioned screenshot, pricing clarity, low-friction trial, onboarding copy.
- **Mistakes:** feature lists, jargon, "all-in-one" claims, hiding pricing.

### 21. Fintech and payments
- **Psychology:** fear of losing money or being scammed; regulation matters.
- **Angles:** security, licensing, speed, fee transparency, integration with mobile money and banks, settlement times, reliability.
- **Proof:** regulatory licences/partners (only if real), uptime stats, transaction volumes, named partners.
- **Rules:** clear fee disclosure; no guaranteed returns; follow financial regulator advertising rules; highlight consumer protection.

### 22. Courses, edtech, digital downloads
- **Psychology:** fear of wasting money and time; scepticism from "guru" fatigue.
- **Angles:** specific outcome and timeframe, curriculum transparency, instructor credibility, community, certificates, sample lessons, small instalments, alignment to local exams or jobs.
- **Proof:** student outcomes, samples, previews, ratings, instructor background.
- **Musts:** who it is for/not for, exactly what is inside, previews, refund policy, support channel, how files are delivered.
- **Rules:** only sell content you have rights to; honest income/results claims. If a digital library depends on copied copyrighted material, tell the owner about the legal and payment-provider risk and suggest licensed, open or original content.

### 23. Freelancers, agencies, consultants
- **Psychology:** buyers fear picking the wrong provider and scope creep.
- **Angles:** specialisation (industry + outcome), productised packages with fixed price and timeline, case studies, process clarity, guarantee, fast response.
- **Musts:** named packages, 3-step process, portfolio with outcomes, "who I'm not for", CTA to a short call or audit.
- **Mistakes:** "full-service", hourly pricing without value, vague portfolio.

### 24. Creators and newsletters
- **Angles:** unique perspective, consistent value, community identity, sample issues, reader proof.
- **Musts:** "Join X readers who get [specific benefit] every [day]", one clear sample, easy subscribe.

### 25. B2B and enterprise
- **Psychology:** committee decisions, risk, ROI, compliance, procurement.
- **Angles:** cost of the problem per month, risk reduction, integration, support, references, pilot offers.
- **Proof:** case studies with numbers, ROI calculators, security/compliance documents, references.
- **Musts:** separate messages for economic buyer, champion, technical evaluator and blocker; a one-page business case; a low-risk pilot.
- **Channels:** outreach, referrals, content, events; see 08.

### 26. Nonprofit and crowdfunding
- **Angles:** specific need and outcome (what one donation does), true urgency, transparency, progress updates, beneficiary dignity.
- **Musts:** one story + one number + one ask; clear fund use; receipts and updates; matching gifts where available.

### 27. Delivery and on-demand services
- **Angles:** speed, reliability, tracking, price clarity, safety, vetted providers, coverage area.
- **Musts:** ETA promises you can keep, price breakdown, refund/compensation rule, safety info, one-tap ordering.

## Hybrid

### 28. Omnichannel pattern (physical + online)
- Make **physical proof** visible online (address, photos, hours, staff, map pin) and **online ease** visible offline (QR to WhatsApp, pay by MoMo, delivery).
- Use the same promise and offer name everywhere.
- Capture contact numbers in-store with consent; follow up on WhatsApp with offers, reminders and review requests.
- Track source with unique numbers, codes ("Say 'RADIO'") or QR codes per channel.

## More verticals (quick entries)

**SACCOs, microfinance, savings groups:** trust and transparency first. Angles: clear rates and fees, member stories, community ownership, easy access, financial education. Proof: registration/licence details, audited reports, member counts, loan examples with total repayment. Rules: show total cost of borrowing; no guaranteed returns; follow financial regulation.

**Cleaning, laundry, home services:** angles: reliability, vetted staff, time-slot windows, before/after, satisfaction redo. Proof: photos, reviews, supervisor accountability. Musts: transparent packages, booking by WhatsApp, safety and trust details for entering homes.

**Printing, branding, signage shops:** angles: speed, quality proofs, bulk pricing, design help. Proof: portfolio by category, turnaround examples. Musts: sample pricing table, file requirements, pickup/delivery, proof approval step.

**Solar, energy, water solutions:** psychology: high cost, fear of poor products and no after-sales. Angles: total cost savings, warranties, local servicing, financing/pay-as-you-go, power-cut resilience. Proof: installations, certifications, warranty terms, references. Musts: transparent specs, savings calculator, maintenance plan.

**Security services and facilities:** angles: trained, vetted, accountable guards; response times; supervision and reporting. Proof: licences, client references, training. Musts: scope clarity, incident process, pricing models.

**Import/export, wholesale, distribution:** angles: reliable supply, consistent quality, credit terms, logistics visibility. Proof: references, product samples, documentation. Musts: MOQ, price tiers, lead times, payment terms, documents provided.

**Pet, vet and animal health:** angles: caring expertise, emergency access, preventive plans. Proof: qualifications, photos, reviews. Rules: no unproven treatment claims.

**Media, entertainment, events promoters:** angles: audience data, past event results, sponsor value. Proof: attendance numbers, partner logos, photo and video highlights. Musts: sponsorship tiers, calendar, contact.

**Political, advocacy and civic campaigns:** neutral and factual guidance only; follow the election and advertising rules; avoid misinformation and divisive manipulation; disclose sponsors; prioritise clarity, accountability, and respect.
