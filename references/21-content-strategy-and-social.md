## 1. Content strategy basics

Content is not "posting more". It is a system: **audience -> promise -> pillars -> formats -> channels -> cadence -> calls to action -> measurement.**
- Define **who you want to attract** and **what they should believe or do** after consuming the content.
- Content should **move people along the journey** (see `15-conversion-audit-and-funnels.md`).
- **Consistency beats intensity**: sustainable rhythm over bursts.
- **Own the audience** (list, WhatsApp channel, email) not just rented platforms.

## 2. Pillars and the 70/20/10 mix

Choose 3 to 5 pillars, such as:
1. **Proof:** customers, results, before/after, reviews, behind-the-scenes outcomes.
2. **Education:** tips, how-tos, myth-busting, FAQs, comparisons.
3. **Process/behind the scenes:** how it is made or done; people; standards.
4. **Offers and updates:** products, prices, availability, events.
5. **Personality and community:** founder voice, team, culture, local life, customer features.

Mix guide: **70 percent value** (education, proof, entertainment), **20 percent engagement/personality**, **10 percent direct promotion.** Adjust to the audience and sales cycle; retail may lean more on offers, B2B more on education and proof.

## 3. The content calendar (30-day template)

Plan weekly themes and daily formats:

| Week | Theme | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trust (who we are) | Founder intro | Process video | Testimonial | FAQ | Offer | Behind the scenes | Community post |
| 2 | Education | Tip 1 | Myth vs fact | Demo | Tip 2 | Offer | Customer story | Q&A |
| 3 | Proof | Before/after | Review spotlight | How it works | Case study | Offer | Team feature | Poll |
| 4 | Offer push | Problem post | Solution reveal | Objection answer | Deadline | Last call | Recap | Thank you |

For each cell, write: hook, format, asset needed, CTA, and tracking code. Batch production (film or shoot in one session), schedule posts, and leave room for timely content.

## 4. Hooks library

Use as starting patterns (fill with specifics):
- "Stop doing [common mistake] if you want [result]."
- "I [did surprising thing] and here is what happened."
- "[Number] signs your [thing] is [problem]."
- "What [customer type] wishes they knew before [action]."
- "The cheapest way to [result] (it's not [obvious option])."
- "Nobody tells you this about [category]."
- "If you [situation], watch this."
- "[Counterintuitive claim] - here's why."
- "POV: [relatable scene]."
- "Here's how much [thing] really costs in [place]."
- "3 questions to ask any [provider] before paying."
- "[Before] -> [After] in [time]."
- "Why [popular belief] is costing you [money/time]."
- "Day [number] of [process]."
- "Reply [WORD] and I'll send [free thing]."
Rules: first 1 to 3 seconds on video; first line on text; match the payoff; avoid bait that disappoints.

## 5. Platform playbooks (short)

**Facebook:** groups, Pages, Marketplace, community posts, events, Stories; longer text works; local groups drive sales; reply to comments fast.
**Instagram:** Reels (hook + value + CTA), carousels (step-by-step, save-worthy), Stories (polls, behind the scenes, links), Highlights as a mini website; strong visual identity.
**TikTok:** fast hook, trend-aware but on-brand, native editing, captions, authentic voice; use comments as content sources; replies-with-video.
**YouTube:** search-driven tutorials and reviews, chapters, strong thumbnails and titles, descriptions with links, Shorts for discovery.
**X/Twitter:** conversation, news, concise insights, threads for teaching; good for B2B and media.
**LinkedIn:** professional stories, lessons, hiring, case studies, B2B outreach; avoid clichés and engagement bait.
**WhatsApp (Status, Channels, groups, broadcast):** see `06` and `17`; treat as relationship channels.
**Pinterest** (where relevant): evergreen visual search for fashion, home, food, weddings.
**Google Business Profile posts:** offers, events, updates for local search.

## 6. Community and UGC

- **Community** = people with shared identity or goals interacting; examples: WhatsApp groups for customers, student circles, VIP clubs, Facebook groups.
- Give members **a reason to join, rules, rituals, recognition, and value** (early access, tips, discounts, peer help).
- **UGC (user-generated content):** invite customers to share (photos, reviews, unboxings); **ask permission**; create a simple branded hashtag or code; feature and thank contributors.
- **Challenges and contests:** simple mechanics, clear rules, platform compliance, real prizes.
- **Moderate** kindly; protect privacy; handle disputes publicly with calm and privately with care.

## 7. Influencers and partnerships

- Choose **micro-influencers and community figures** who are trusted by the target audience; check engagement, audience location, and authenticity.
- **Brief them** with the audience, promise, must-say points, claims they cannot make, tracking code/link, disclosure requirements, and creative freedom.
- Offer **fair compensation** (cash, products, commission).
- Co-marketing with **complementary businesses** (salon + photographer, shop + delivery, school + uniform supplier): shared offers, cross-promotion.
- **Affiliate/referral programmes:** clear rewards and rules; track properly.
- Disclose paid relationships as required.

## 8. PR and thought leadership

- **Newsworthy angles:** a first, a number, a local story, a trend, a human story, a controversy handled responsibly, a community impact.
- **Pitch** short, specific, and relevant to the journalist or radio host; provide quotes, data, images, and a contact.
- **Press kit:** about, founder bio, product images, facts, logos, contact.
- **Thought leadership:** consistent perspective, original data or experience, honest opinions; publish on LinkedIn, blogs, newsletters, local media, and speaking.
- **Crisis communication:** acknowledge fast, state facts, take responsibility where due, explain actions, and provide updates; avoid defensiveness.

## 9. Repurposing system

One **pillar piece** feeds many assets:
- Long video or article -> 5 short clips -> 3 quote graphics -> carousel -> newsletter -> WhatsApp status -> FAQ entries -> email sequence -> poster text.
- Customer interview -> case study -> testimonial cards -> ad creative -> sales script proof.
- Keep a **content library** with tags (topic, stage, format) so you can reuse and refresh.

## 10. Social customer care

- Reply quickly and politely to comments and DMs; move sensitive issues to private channels.
- Template responses for common questions, but personalise.
- Turn **complaints into proof** by resolving publicly and asking for an updated review when appropriate.
- Escalate legal or safety issues to a human decision-maker.
- Track common questions to feed FAQs and content ideas.

## 11. Measurement and iteration

- **Reach and engagement** indicate resonance; **saves, shares, replies, and DMs** signal value; **clicks, calls, WhatsApp chats, and sales** signal business impact.
- Review weekly: top performers, flops, audience questions, and what to repeat.
- Record learnings in the learning log (`10-quality-and-testing.md`).
- Do not chase vanity metrics; tie each content type to a business goal.

## 12. Content for different business types (quick prompts)

- **Restaurant:** dish close-ups, kitchen reveals, price specials, chef intro, customer reactions.
- **Salon:** before/after, hair care tips, product reviews, booking tips.
- **Shop:** new arrivals, price lists, "how to choose", unboxing, customer pickups.
- **Clinic:** myth-busting, prevention tips, staff introductions (follow rules).
- **School:** student achievements, teacher spotlights, parent testimonials, open day.
- **SaaS/app:** product tips, user stories, release notes, behind-the-scenes engineering, templates.
- **Agency/freelancer:** case studies, before/after, process, lessons learned.
- **Agribusiness:** field updates, harvest stories, price reports, how-to guides.
- **Real estate:** walkthroughs, neighbourhood guides, buying process explainers, scam warnings.
